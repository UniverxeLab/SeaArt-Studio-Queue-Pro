Frequently Asked Questions

Does this extension send my prompts, images, or SeaArt cookies to remote servers?

No. Everything runs 100% inside your own Chrome browser using Manifest V3 local storage (chrome.storage.local) and Chrome's native downloads API. We operate zero remote scraping servers and never see your prompts, SeaArt account, or generated media. The only external request is optional Pro license validation directly against api.lemonsqueezy.com if you enter a key.

How is billing handled and who processes Pro payments?

Paid licenses ($4.90/mo Starter Pro and $29 Early-Bird Lifetime) are sold directly by UniverxeLab through Lemon Squeezy as Merchant of Record, not by Google or Chrome Web Store. Lemon Squeezy handles cards/PayPal, regional VAT/sales tax compliance, receipts, and instant email license key delivery.

What if SeaArt.ai updates its button layout and selectors stop clicking?

Open the Setup & Pro tab and check Selector Health. Any affected box turns amber/red and you can click Pick next to that control, then click the corresponding field on SeaArt.ai to save a permanent local override in 5 seconds.

No. SeaArt Studio Queue & Bulk Downloader is an independent client-side productivity utility developed by UniverxeLab to help creators organize prompts and archives inside their own browser session. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by SeaArt.ai.
